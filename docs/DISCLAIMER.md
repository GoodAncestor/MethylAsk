# About These Results

MethylAsk reports **research associations** between methylation markers and
traits or conditions, drawn from public scientific databases. It does not
diagnose, treat, or predict disease.

- Each finding carries an **evidence tier** — robust, moderate, speculative, or
  unknown. A tier describes how much scientific support exists for an
  association. It is not a statement about your health.
- Associations are population-level and are often specific to a tissue or
  ancestry. A marker being present does not mean an outcome will occur.
- These results are **not a substitute for professional medical advice**.
  Anyone considering a health decision should consult a qualified clinician who
  can see their full context.
- The underlying databases are updated over time. A report reflects the database
  versions recorded in its footer at the moment it was generated.

This is the single source of disclaimer text for MethylAsk. The web interface
links to it, the report embeds it once, and the command-line tool points to it.
It is not restated across code comments or scattered through the interface.
