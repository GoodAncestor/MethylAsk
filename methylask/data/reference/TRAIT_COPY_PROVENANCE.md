## Traits with thin methylation-specific literature

These traits are included in `trait_copy.json` (each carries at least a review or narrower single-cohort source), but the underlying methylation-specific literature is thin relative to well-studied traits like smoking or age. Per the brief, this is reported as a real finding, not padded.

- **Body Fat Percentage** (`body_fat`): Body fat percentage (as distinct from BMI or waist measures) has comparatively little dedicated methylation literature; searches surfaced one large single-cohort EWAS (Generation Scotland, PMID 39706196) and one twin study (PMID 37392072), but no independent multi-cohort meta-analysis specific to body fat percentage was found.
- **Waist-Hip Ratio** (`waist_hip_ratio`): WHR methylation studies are smaller and less cross-replicated than BMI studies; most available evidence comes from single-population cohorts (Korean, Chinese twins) plus one older 4-cohort European meta-analysis (PMID 29713043), rather than a large trans-ethnic consortium equivalent to the BMI or HDL literature.
- **Gestational Age at Birth** (`gestational_age`): Searched for dedicated epigenetic-gestational-age review/meta-analysis papers (e.g. 'epigenetic gestational age clock systematic review', 'placental epigenetic clock gestational age review'); none were found. The literature instead consists of predictor-development and cross-cohort validation papers in Clinical Epigenetics (Haftorn, Deng, Yaskolka Meir), which is why copy cites these rather than a review/meta-analysis and notes the smaller, newer evidence base.
- **Eosinophilia (Elevated Eosinophil Count)** (`eosinophilia`): Searched PubMed for 'eosinophil count epigenome-wide association', 'eosinophilia methylation biomarker', and blood-cell-composition deconvolution terms. No dedicated large-scale EWAS meta-analysis of eosinophil count as a standalone quantitative trait was found; most relevant evidence comes from (a) foundational papers showing eosinophils/granulocytes have distinct methylation profiles that confound whole-blood EWAS, and (b) a single family-cohort study (NTR, van Dongen 2024) where a methylation-associated component loaded on eosinophil counts within a broader asthma-marker analysis. Flagging as thin literature.
- **Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma** (`pancreatic_ductal_adenocarcinoma`): Searched for EWAS/prospective blood methylation studies of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma; the great majority of available methylation literature is tumor-tissue or cell-free DNA biomarker work done after diagnosis. Only one prospective, pre-diagnosis blood EWAS (nested case-control across 3 cohorts, PMID 33134824) was found, so population-level pre-diagnosis evidence is thin relative to the tumor-tissue literature.
- **Chronic Pain** (`chronic_pain`): Searched broadly for chronic pain / chronic widespread pain / fibromyalgia / chronic low back pain EWAS. The evidence base is small and concentrated in one twin cohort (TwinsUK) for chronic widespread pain, with only a few small, largely unreplicated case-control studies for other pain subtypes (e.g. chronic low back pain, n<100). This is genuinely a thin, early-stage literature relative to fields like smoking or age.
